This is a place for practical notes from real projects—what held up, what didn’t, and what I’d do again.

If you’re responsible for a business website, an internal tool, or the systems behind them, the goal is to share patterns that reduce friction and help things run calmer.

## What you’ll find here

- Short after-action notes (what changed, what improved)
- Tradeoffs explained plainly (not as ideology)
- Checklists and small playbooks you can reuse

## What you won’t find

- Inflated claims
- Cargo-cult best practices
- Process for its own sake
