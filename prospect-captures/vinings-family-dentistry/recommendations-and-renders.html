Mini Deck

Slide 1 · Core diagnosis

The practice feels more credible than the website.

  • Current site feels older and more template-driven than the business likely is.
  • The trust ingredients are already there — they’re just weakly packaged.
  • The biggest practical opportunities are homepage clarity, about-page trust framing, and appointment flow cleanup.
Slide 2 · What to update first

Suggested first-pass scope

  • Refresh homepage first impression and CTA hierarchy
  • Rebuild the about/practice section around structured trust
  • Simplify the appointment flow and remove friction/error risk
  • Shorten and clarify the mobile experience
Slide 3 · Why this matters commercially

This is mostly a trust + conversion problem, not a content problem.

  • The practice already has services, credibility, and local identity.
  • The site’s design language makes all of that feel less current than it should.
  • A focused refresh could improve first impression without needing a giant rebuild.
Slide 4 · Mobile angle

Mobile makes the template limitations more obvious.

  • The current site compresses poorly on smaller screens.
  • First-screen clarity and CTA priority matter even more on mobile.
  • A lighter mobile hierarchy is an easy credibility win.
