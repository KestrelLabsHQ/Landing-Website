## Template notes

### Keep
- one observation
- one opportunity framing statement
- one low-pressure offer
- grounded signature

### Avoid
- sounding like a pitch deck
- overexplaining the brand
- promising outcomes too aggressively
- immediate meeting ask in the first touch
- criticism-heavy framing
