# Kestrel One-Shot Prospect Analyzer

Use this when you want a fast, repeatable prospect review from minimal inputs.

## How to use
Copy the prompt below into OpenClaw (or another capable agent) and replace the placeholders.

---

## Copy/Paste Prompt

```text
Analyze this prospect for Kestrel Labs and produce a concise, high-signal lead brief.

Business name: <business name>
Website: <url>
Google Maps: <url or none>
LinkedIn: <url or none>
Instagram: <url or none>
Facebook: <url or none>
Other links: <url list or none>
My notes: <optional>

Requirements:
- Use only visible/publicly attributable information from the provided links and anything directly observable from them.
- Do not invent contact details, business facts, or capabilities.
- If something is uncertain, say so explicitly.
- Keep the tone aligned with Kestrel Labs: selective, practical, founder-led, understated.
- Prefer concrete findings over abstract branding commentary.

Return the output in this exact structure:

1. Prospect Snapshot
- Business name
- Category / type
- Apparent location
- What they appear to do

2. First Impression
- What feels strong
- What feels weak
- Whether the business seems more credible than the website suggests

3. Website / Digital Findings
- Messaging / positioning issues
- Credibility / trust issues
- Contact / intake friction
- Technical / UX roughness
- Signs the site may be underselling the business

4. Business Quality Signals
- Professionalism
- Reputation / maturity signals
- Likely budget / seriousness

5. Kestrel Fit Assessment
- Pursue / Maybe / Skip
- 3-6 bullets explaining why

6. Best First Offer
Choose one:
- Website Credibility Refresh
- Contact & Intake Cleanup
- Lightweight Internal Tool
- Technical Cleanup / Stabilization
- Not enough evidence

Then explain why that is the best first wedge.

7. Suggested Path Forward
Give the practical next step.

8. Ballpark Budget Range
Give an order-of-magnitude estimate in USD and a short reason.

9. Outreach Tailoring
Provide:
- 2 subject line options
- 1 outreach angle summary
- 1 personalized first outreach email draft

10. Internal Summary for CRM
Give a short block with:
- one-line summary
- likely offer
- priority
- next step

Additional guidance:
- If the lead looks weak, say so.
- If you need more info to be confident, say exactly what is missing.
- Keep the outreach draft human and restrained, not agency-like.
- Optimize for a useful first pass that helps me decide quickly whether to pursue.
```

---

## Suggested usage pattern
- Use this for 1 lead at a time
- Review the output manually before sending anything
- Paste the CRM summary into your lead tracker
- If the lead is strong, ask for a second pass that tightens only the email draft

## Best for
- website-led outreach
- local-business prospecting
- trust-heavy professional services
- contractor / clinic / accounting / legal leads
