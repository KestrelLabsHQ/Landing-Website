# Credibility + Intake Review

A fixed-scope review for clinics and trust-heavy local practices whose website may be underselling the business or making the next step harder than it should be.

## Best fit

This offer is best for practices like:
- physical therapy
- chiropractic
- sports rehab / performance
- massage / recovery
- similar appointment-driven local care businesses

It is meant for situations where:
- the business appears real and established
- the website feels weaker than the business itself
- trust signals are present but not packaged well
- booking, contact, or new-patient flow feels clunky
- there may be a practical systems/tooling issue behind the front-end friction

## What this is

This is not a giant strategy project.
It is a short, evidence-based review focused on the pages and flows that shape first impression, trust, and next-step behavior.

The goal is simple:
- show what is helping
- show what is creating friction
- identify the highest-leverage fixes
- clarify whether the next move should be a website refresh, intake cleanup, scheduling/tooling change, or a small internal workflow improvement

## Fixed scope

Kestrel Labs will review up to 5 core pages / experiences:
- homepage
- one primary service page
- provider / team / about page
- new patient / intake page (if present)
- contact / booking page

Review includes desktop and mobile inspection.

## Deliverables

Client receives:
- a concise written review
- 6-10 screenshots with callouts
- the top issues ranked by practical impact
- a short "2-week plan" and "4-week plan"
- one recommended next engagement path, such as:
  - website credibility refresh
  - intake / scheduling cleanup
  - lightweight internal workflow improvement
  - not enough evidence / no project recommended

## What the review focuses on

- first-impression credibility
- provider / team trust presentation
- clarity of services and specialties
- new-patient reassurance and clarity
- booking / contact friction
- mobile usability in the first key screens
- signs that the real business is stronger than the website suggests
- obvious tooling or workflow roughness behind the scenes

## What this does not include

Unless separately agreed, this review does not include:
- copywriting implementation
- design files
- code changes
- full SEO audit
- ad strategy
- analytics setup
- compliance review by counsel
- storage or handling of protected health information

## HIPAA / PHI note

If a follow-on project touches patient data, forms, messages, intake records, or other protected health information, that work must be scoped around HIPAA-appropriate tooling, vendors, and agreements.

This review itself should be kept on the public-facing / operational side and should not require Kestrel Labs to access or store patient data.
