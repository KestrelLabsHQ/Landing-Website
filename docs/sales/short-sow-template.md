# Short SOW / Service Agreement Template

> Practical template for the fixed-price Credibility + Intake Review.
> Review with counsel before using as a standard legal document.

## Parties

This Statement of Work / Service Agreement ("Agreement") is between:

- **Provider:** Kestrel Labs LLC ("Kestrel")
- **Client:** <Client legal name>

Effective date: <date>

## Service

Kestrel will perform a fixed-scope **Credibility + Intake Review** of the Client's public-facing website and related public booking / contact / intake experience.

The review is intended to identify practical opportunities to improve trust, clarity, first impression, and next-step flow.

## Scope

Kestrel will review up to five (5) key public-facing pages / experiences, such as:
- homepage
- one primary service page
- provider / team / about page
- new patient / intake page
- contact / booking page

Kestrel may use browser inspection, screenshots, page captures, and written analysis as part of the work.

## Deliverables

Kestrel will provide:
- one concise written review
- approximately 6-10 screenshots or visual callouts
- a prioritized list of key issues / opportunities
- a short recommended path forward

Unless otherwise stated, deliverables are provided as a PDF, markdown document, or similar written format.

## Timeline

Kestrel will deliver the review within **2 business days** after:
1. this Agreement is accepted,
2. required kickoff information is received, and
3. payment is received.

## Fee

Fixed fee: **$1,500 USD**

Payment terms:
- 100% due before work begins

## Client responsibilities

Client agrees to:
- provide the correct website / URLs to review
- identify any specific public-facing concerns they want considered
- respond promptly to reasonable kickoff questions

## Out of scope

Unless separately agreed in writing, this engagement does **not** include:
- implementation work
- copywriting or design production
- code changes
- analytics setup
- advertising strategy
- legal or compliance review
- handling, storage, or processing of protected health information

## HIPAA / PHI limitation

This engagement is intended to stay on the public-facing side of the business.
Kestrel is not being engaged under this Agreement to receive, store, process, or manage protected health information.

If future work would involve PHI, that work must be separately scoped with appropriate tooling, controls, and agreements.

## Intellectual property

Upon full payment, Client may use the delivered review internally for planning and decision-making.
Kestrel retains ownership of its background methods, templates, frameworks, and know-how.

## Confidentiality

Each party will use reasonable care to protect non-public information shared during the engagement and will not disclose it except as needed to perform the work or as required by law.

## No guarantee of business results

Kestrel does not guarantee a particular increase in leads, bookings, revenue, or patient volume.
The review is a professional assessment intended to support decision-making.

## Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Kestrel's liability arising out of this engagement will not exceed the amount paid under this Agreement.
