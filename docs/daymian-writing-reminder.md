# Daymian — 5-Bullet Writing Reminder

Use this before sending anything important.

1. **Say the point plainly.**  
   Don’t decorate it to make it sound smarter.

2. **Sound like a real operator, not a polished marketer.**  
   Useful beats elegant.

3. **Lead with upside, not criticism.**  
   Show the opportunity without putting the other person on the defensive.

4. **Keep it specific, low-pressure, and commercially clear.**  
   One real observation is enough.

5. **If it sounds fake, over-polished, or mass-produced, cut it.**  
   Your edge is sounding real.
